The five-tier risk scale

Every alert on TrailHead is rated on the same scale, so a rating means the same thing on every route.

Low

Generally safe travel

Normal caution advised. Conditions are stable across the route.

Moderate

Evaluate terrain carefully

Heightened avalanche conditions on specific terrain features.

Consid.

Guide judgement required

Dangerous avalanche conditions; route adjustments likely on site.

High

Route sections closed

Very dangerous conditions; affected sections closed until reassessed.

What to do when an alert changes

1

Check the route page

Current risk level is shown at the top of every route listing before you book or depart.

2

Message your guide

Guides can confirm whether a scheduled departure is affected and what's changed.

3

Follow the alternate plan

Every route has a lower-risk alternate section briefed in advance for exactly this case.

4

Call the emergency line

The 24-hour line connects active bookings directly to on-call guide support.
