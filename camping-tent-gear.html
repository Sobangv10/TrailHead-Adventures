What to expect

01

Season-rated tents

Every tent lists its season rating and pack weight, so a 3-season shelter isn't mistaken for 4-season gear.

02

Temperature-rated bags

Sleeping bags list a comfort and limit temperature rating, not just a vague 'warm' or 'cold' label.

03

Right-sized cookware

Cookware sets are offered by group size, from solo setups to 6-person group kits.

04

Inspected every rental

Every item is checked for wear and re-certified before it's handed out again.

Frequently asked

Anything item-specific is answered on the individual listing.

What if a tent leaks on the trip?

Report it at drop-off — every returned item is inspected and pulled from rotation if faulty.

Can I rent for just one night?

Yes, there's no reduced-rate penalty for a single-night rental.

Is cookware cleaned between rentals?

Yes, all cookware is washed and inspected within the 24-hour turnaround window.
