Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions before hitting the trail? Here is how our rental process keeps your expedition seamless.

How do pickup and returns work for full packages?

Your entire package is bundled in one heavy-duty duffel bag. Pick it up at your selected trailhead desk, complete a 2-minute condition check, and drop it off at any partner drop-off point after your trip.

Can I swap individual item sizes inside a package?

Yes! After reserving your package, you can specify sizing for boots, backpacks, and apparel through your online account up to 24 hours before pickup.

Are satellite beacons included in every package?

GPS satellite messengers are included standard in Alpine Summit and River Expedition packages, and can be added as an optional extra for short weekend trips.
