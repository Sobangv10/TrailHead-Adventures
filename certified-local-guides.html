Guides who live near the route

TrailHead guides are based in the regions they lead in, not flown in for the season. Profiles list years guiding, certifications held and the specific routes each guide runs most often.

Wilderness first aid certification re-checked annually

Safety is our priority. Every trail guide's wilderness first aid certification is re-checked each year, so you're in capable hands on every adventure.

Route specialities listed per guide profile

Know your guide's strengths. Each profile highlights the routes they run most, from alpine treks to wildlife trails — so you choose the right guide for your journey.

Guide-to-group ratio capped, never overbooked

Smaller groups for a better experience — strict guide-to-group ratios ensure you get more attention, safety and a truly immersive journey.
