What's in a TrailHead kit

Every guided expedition carries a kit built around these four categories.

01

Wound care

Dressings, antiseptic wipes, and pressure bandages for bleeding control.

02

Splinting & support

Compact splints and wraps for sprains and suspected fractures.

03

Environmental protection

Emergency blankets and hand warmers for exposure and cold injuries.

04

Signal & rescue

GPS beacon, whistle and signal mirror carried on every multi-day route.

If something goes wrong on trail

1

Stop and assess

Check the scene for ongoing hazards before approaching the injured person.

2

Stabilise

Address bleeding, breathing and shock before anything else.

3

Decide: stay or move

Weigh self-evacuation against signalling and waiting, based on the injury and terrain.

4

Call the emergency line

The 24-hour line connects active bookings directly to on-call guide support.
