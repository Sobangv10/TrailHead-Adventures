Height and load, stated plainly

Every treehouse lists its deck height, access method and rated load capacity, so there are no surprises about the climb or the bridge crossing once you arrive.

How the 42 treehouses are accessed

Rope-bridge 48%
Wooden staircase 38%

01

Load capacity checked

Every deck is load-rated and re-inspected annually, listed on the booking page.

02

Canopy height listed

Deck height above ground is stated per listing, from 3 metres to 18 metres.

03

Wind-rated structures

Treehouses are rated for local wind conditions and closed automatically above safe limits.

04

Trailhead proximity

Most treehouses sit within a short walk of at least one guided expedition start point.

Booking a treehouse

1

Check access & height

Review the listed access method and canopy height before booking, especially with kids.

2

Confirm load capacity

Each listing states max guest weight and deck load rating.

3

Most treehouses provide bedding and lantern lighting — check the listing for what's supplied.
