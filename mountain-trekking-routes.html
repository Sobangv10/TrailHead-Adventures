What to expect

Four things every mountain route carries, whichever ridge you pick.

01

Ridge-line briefings

Every group gets a route and weather briefing the evening before departure.

02

Acclimatisation built in

Multi-day routes include rest days scheduled around elevation gain, not just distance.

03

Capped group size

No more than 10 trekkers per guide on standard routes, 6 on technical alpine sections.

04

Emergency descent plans

Every route has a mapped rapid-descent option briefed before departure.

How a trekking route runs

1

Book & brief

Reserve your date, then join a video briefing on gear and route conditions a week out.

2

Trailhead check-in

Meet your guide at the trailhead for a gear check and route walkthrough.

3

Guided trekking days

Trek in a capped group, with acclimatisation days built into longer routes.

4

Descent & debrief

Descend via the mapped route with a short debrief and condition report for the next group.
