Frequently asked questions

Do I need a satellite subscription?

No. Active emergency connectivity and monitoring services are included standard with your daily rental rate.

What's in the first aid kit?

Kits include trauma bandages, wound care, splints, and blister treatments itemized to match your group size.

Can I load my own custom GPX route?

Yes! Simply upload your custom GPX file during checkout and our technicians will sync it to the device before pickup.
