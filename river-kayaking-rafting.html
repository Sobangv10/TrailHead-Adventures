What to expect on the water

Grade-matched groups ★ Daily water checks ★ Full safety kit included ★ Rescue-certified guides ★

Grade-matched groups

Paddlers are grouped strictly by prior experience so a Grade IV run never mixes inconsistent skill levels.

Daily water checks

Guides confirm water flow and active grade classifications each morning before departure.

Full safety kit included

High-buoyancy life jackets, helmets, and throw bags are fully standard issue for everyone.

Rescue-certified guides

All river guides hold international swiftwater rescue certifications, updated annually.

Four river experiences

Select an expedition suited to your group's experience level.

1 Day - Grade IV

Whitewater Rapids Run

A fast, technical run through a narrow limestone gorge designed for seekers of high adrenaline.

Half Day - Grade I

Sunset Calm-Water Paddle

An easy evening paddle suited to complete beginners, families, and relaxing scenic tours.

2 Days - Grade III

Canyon Gorge Rafting

A two-day raft descent through a steep-walled canyon with wilderness riverside camping overnight.

5 Days - Grade II–III

Multi-Day River Expedition

A comprehensive five-day descent with complete gear support and riverside wilderness camping.

How a river trip runs

From the moment you step foot at the base camp to taking out downstream, we follow a precise sequence for safety and enjoyment.

1

Grade confirmation

Guides check water levels the morning of departure and confirm conditions directly with the group.

2

Safety briefing

A mandatory briefing on paddling technique, signals, and swiftwater rescue positions before launch.

3

On the water

Guided paddling in grade-matched groups with strategic rest stops along scenic riverbanks.

4

Take-out & debrief

A short team debrief at take-out covering route highlights and returning gear.

Frequently asked

A swim test is required for Grade III routes and above; the sunset paddle has no swim requirement as waters are calm and life jackets are provided.

High-buoyancy life jackets, safety helmets, and paddles are included on every route; full wetsuits and booties can be added during online checkout.

Guides assess real-time river gauges daily and will substitute a grade-appropriate alternative route or reschedule rather than run unsafe water.
