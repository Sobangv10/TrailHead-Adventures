Frequently asked

What if boots don't fit at pickup?

Staff can swap sizes on the spot at most trailhead pickup points, provided local stock allows.

Do I need to wash gear before returning?

No, all rented apparel is professionally sanitized and cleaned as part of the turn-in process.

Are waterproof ratings tested?

Yes, each shell's waterproof rating is lab-verified before re-entering rotation.
