Frequently asked

Still not sure? Guides at the trailhead can answer route-specific safety questions before you set out.

Are all guides first-aid certified?

Yes, every listed guide holds a current wilderness first aid certification, renewed annually.

Do GPX files update after a reroute?

Yes, downloads are refreshed whenever a guide reports a permanent reroute or closure.

How current are the weather alerts?

Alerts are monitored continuously and scoped to the specific route rather than a broad regional area.
